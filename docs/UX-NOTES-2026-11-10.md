# Walkthrough notes, 2026-11-10

Soul Forge Test (dev build, test flavour) with Chris's library (25 characters, from the release app's backup of
2026-11-06) and the test content (Aurora + 5e.tools). Used as a player would: Home, opening characters, Build, Play,
Manage, Export, the content switch and "Move characters to 5e.tools".

Legend: **Bug** (fixed in this pass, or listed if not), **Check** (looked into), **For Chris** (would change design; not
changed without your review).

## Bugs

1. **Fixed. A Tortle in armor showed AC 10 with no warning** (Carambo Portman, Play). The Tortle's shell gives AC 17
   only unarmored, and a Tortle can't wear light, medium or heavy armor; Carambo has Leather Armor equipped, so the
   sheet showed 11 - 1 = 10. The engine is right to use the armor, but nothing told the player. The rules check now
   says: "Armor the species cannot wear: Natural Armor: Leather Armor is worn; without it, armor class is 17" (any
   species whose trait says it can't wear armor). Carambo's sheet itself is unchanged until the armor is unequipped.
2. **Fixed. Play header listed the same DC twice** ("WIS DC 11 · WIS DC 11", Carambo: Druid and Magic Initiate cast
   with Wisdom). Identical entries now show once.
3. **Fixed. Spell saves without their DC** (Spells tab): Entangle showed "DC 11 STR save", but Infestation, Charm
   Person and Blindness/Deafness only "CON save" / "WIS save", because the DC was read from the text and only some
   spells say "your spell save DC". A spell's save now always shows the caster's spell save DC.
4. **Fixed. Opening a character marked it "Edited just now"** in the library, with nothing changed (the autosave ran on
   open). Now only a real change is saved and dated; a new, duplicated or imported character still saves at once.
5. **Fixed (5e.tools content). Heavy armor without its Strength did not slow the character**: Paulie (Strength 8, chain
   mail) moved 30 feet on 5e.tools content, 20 under Aurora. Converted heavy armor now costs 10 feet below its
   Strength, unless the character ignores it (the Dwarf's "speed is not reduced by wearing heavy armor").
6. **Fixed (5e.tools content). The "2024 rules" option for a 2014 background existed only with Aurora loaded**: on
   5e.tools content alone a 2024 species with a 2014 background had no way to get its ability increases and origin
   feat. Built now from 5e.tools' own parts; a 2014 species' increases give way to it as Aurora's do.
7. **Fixed (5e.tools content). The 2024 Elf chose Keen Senses' skill twice** (once for the species, once again for its
   lineage), so every 5e.tools 2024 Elf had one skill too many.
8. **Fixed (test build). The move flagged every character** with "Class not in the rules reference": the reference is
   loaded on demand and was not loaded yet. It is now loaded first, and that finding (about the reference, not the
   character) is not counted. 25 characters with a finding became 4.
9. **Fixed (test build). A moved copy said it "was saved by an older Soul Forge"**: it kept its original's version
   stamp. A copy is stamped with this version.
10. **Fixed (migration). Favorites, uses spent and companion hit points kept Aurora ids**, so Play's favorites read
   "A spell you no longer have" (Waldrom). They now move with the character.
11. **Fixed (migration). "Variant Feature: Origin Feat"** (Aurora's switch, not a pick) showed in the notes as a pick
   no choice takes. Left out.
13. **Fixed. React key warnings on Build** (63 in the console): Build lists feats as chips keyed by their text, so
   four "Ability Score Improvement" chips shared a key (items can then vanish or double on update). Keyed by position.
14. **Fixed (5e.tools content). Card blurbs for older species were their Age line** ("Half-elves mature at the same
   rate humans do"). The card now shows the first paragraph that is not Age, Alignment, Size, Speed, Languages,
   Ability Score Increase or Creature Type.
12. **Open. In 5e.tools-only mode, Aurora characters show "?" and nonsense numbers** on Home (class "?", "HP 0 / -1").
   The content they need is not loaded, which the switch's message says; until they are moved, a card could say
   "needs Aurora content" instead of numbers. Small, test build only today; matters for D7.

## For Chris (design)

1. **An "Unnamed" character is open on a fresh start**, with a Build badge of 3, before the player made one. Home
   then shows the library while the header names a character nobody created. Suggest: no character open until one is
   opened or made.
2. **Opening a character from Home goes to Build**, not Play. For a finished character a player usually wants the
   sheet. Suggest: Play for a character with nothing left to choose, Build otherwise.
3. **Older characters always show two choices left**: "Hit points: choose fixed or rolled" and "Starting equipment"
   (Waldrom: 49 items, "starting gear 0 of 2"; hit points have been "average" all along). These predate both questions.
   Suggest: a character with gear counts its starting equipment as done; a character with a saved hit point mode
   counts that as chosen.
4. **"Import notes" holds the move's account too.** Suggest a neutral name ("Notes on this character's history" or
   "Changes") once the move ships.
5. **Play's tab row wraps** (Conditions and Extras on a second line at 1024 wide).
6. **"Bag of Holding" is the name of the floating-window tray** (bottom left, whenever a pick window is open). On a
   character sheet a D&D magic item's name reads as gear: a new character with no items shows "Bag of Holding: 0 in
   the bag". Suggest a plain name ("Open windows").
7. **Generic options carry a "5etools" source badge** (the Elf's Insight, Perception, Survival). It is not a book;
   suggest no badge, or the book of the element that offers the choice.
8. **"Move characters to 5e.tools" stays in Soul Forge Test.** The release carries the migration's code and every
   fix, but players see the move only with D7 (your go-ahead).
9. **Test build: messages stay until clicked**, and the 5e.tools-only message covered the Background card's button for
   the whole session. Suggest the test messages fade after 10 seconds.

## Redundant ways to the same thing (for Chris)

Collected from every tab, the Menu and Settings. Each is fine alone; together they are more paths than a player needs.

1. **Level up**: Build's bar, Manage's bar, and Play's header icon. Suggest: Build and Play only (Manage is not where a
   player levels up).
2. **Play and Export**: the header tabs, and buttons of the same names on Build's and Manage's bars. Suggest: drop the
   bar buttons; the tabs are always there.
3. **Hit points setup**: Build's Lists drawer ("Hit points: choose fixed or rolled"), Build's Scores panel ("HP …
   Configure"), Play's header ("… Configure") and Play's "Hit point rolls" icon: four doors to one dialog. Suggest:
   Scores on Build and the Play header.
4. **The Export tab repeats Play's tracker**: current and temp HP, death saves, hit dice, experience, Long rest and
   Inspiration sit above the printable sheet. Suggest: Export shows the sheet and the export buttons only.
5. **Back up / restore the library**: Home has "Export library" and "Open file (.json)" (which restores a library),
   Settings has "Back up all characters" and "Restore...". Suggest: one place (Home), Settings keeps only the automatic
   backup switch.
6. **Two import buttons on Home**: "Open file (.json)" and "Import Foundry / Roll20 / Plutonium". The app already tells
   a library from a character file; it could tell a Foundry or Roll20 file too. Suggest: one "Open or import" button.
7. **Report a bug**: in the Menu and at the top of Settings. Suggest: Menu only.
8. **Tutorial and tips**: Menu "Show the tutorial", Settings "Show tips again". Fine if they are different things; the
   names do not say how they differ.
9. **Characters listed twice on Home**: the cards (or compact list) and, below them, the party summary of the same
   characters. Suggest: the party summary as its own view (a third choice next to Cards / Compact).
10. **Rules lookups**: the header's "Search rules" and Content's browse lists (Spells, Items, Feats, Species,
   Backgrounds, Creatures, Classes, Options) cover the same compendium. Fine if one is "find" and the other "manage
   content"; Content's lists could link to search.
11. **Export one character**: Export tab "Export character", the ⤓ on each Home card, and Build's "Export" (which only
   switches to the Export tab).

## Migration (D6) findings on the library

- Resilient (2024): Aurora asks once ("Constitution") for both the +1 and the save; 5e.tools asks twice. The move gave
  Carambo and Talaan a Strength save instead of Constitution (the auto-fill took the first option). Fixed: one pick
  answers its owner's other choices when an option names it.
- The 2024 Ability Score Improvement feat: the move put the same copy in four improvement levels at once, so three
  levels' increases were lost (Waldrom Brahms: 4 ability points, 14 hit points, 1 AC). Fixed: a feat goes to one place
  per round, and the old "+1 to two" shape is kept per level.
- A Wizard's spellbook: Aurora lets the 1st-level choice hold 2nd-level spells (the spellbook is legal as a whole at 3rd
  level); those had nowhere to go, were prepared outside the spellbook and the places got random spells (Dunbar
  Trusterton). Fixed: a spell the finished character can cast takes a place in its own casting's choice.
- The "2024 rules" background option keeps its id (Soul Forge's own element exists in both content sets); Dimothy
  Summers keeps his increases and Alert.
- Library after the fixes: 25 characters, no AC, hit point or speed changes; 3 characters' scores differ (the fill's
  choice of where a +1 goes), the rest are spells and skills 5e.tools gives or names differently, and a few drops with
  their reason (Canaith Mandolin, Chalk: no 5e.tools version; Abyssal, Undercommon, Common Sign Language: the 5e.tools
  choice only takes standard languages; Shape Water, Spare the Dying: not on 5e.tools' 2024 Bard list).
