# Third-party material

Soul Forge's code is MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`). Anyone may use, copy, modify and redistribute it.

Bundled with the app:

- **Fonts** (Cinzel, Cinzel Decorative, Roboto Condensed, Crimson Pro, Alegreya Sans, DejaVu Sans): SIL Open Font License or the DejaVu license, through the `@fontsource` and `dejavu-fonts-ttf` packages.
- **Stock portraits** in `public/portraits/`: public domain or CC0 art. Titles, licenses and source links are in `public/portraits/CREDITS.md`.
- **Spell school icons** in `src/ui/schoolIcons.tsx`: Lorc (magic shield, portal, crystal ball, charm, domino mask, skull and crossed bones, cycle) and Delapouite (fire spell cast) from https://game-icons.net, licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The path data is unchanged.
- **Condition icons** in `src/ui/conditionIcons.tsx`: Skoll (sight disabled, hearts, hearing disabled, knockout), Lorc (terror, grab, despair, manacles, star swirl), Delapouite (invisible, frozen body, stone bust, tired eye) and Sbed (poison cloud, falling) from https://game-icons.net, licensed CC BY 3.0. The path data is unchanged.
- **DLDM character sheets** (5E24 sheets, companion sheets) are made by DLtheDM and are not bundled. Soul Forge links to the creator's shared folder and fills a copy you download yourself, which stays on your computer.
- **Official Wizards of the Coast character sheets** are not bundled, because they are Wizards' files. The desktop app downloads them from Wizards' own addresses when you press Get, and keeps the copy on your computer.

Not bundled, and not covered by the MIT license: Dungeons & Dragons rules content, the official character sheet PDF, and any 5etools data. Soul Forge reads copies you load yourself.
